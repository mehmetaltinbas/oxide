# Fonts

Both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows bundling and
redistribution inside a product like this one, including commercially, so long
as the fonts themselves are not sold on their own and this notice travels with
them.

- **Bangers** by Vernon Adams, Kimberly Geswein, Sol Matas.
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Bangers
- **Comic Neue** by Craig Rozynski.
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Comic+Neue

Self-hosted rather than pulled from Google's CDN: the game should not need a
second domain to be readable, the files are small, and a font that arrives late
means text that reflows after the first frame.

Latin subsets only, which is what the game's text needs and is why these are
kilobytes rather than hundreds of them.
